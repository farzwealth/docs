## Where marks were lost

**Problem Description (-0.5)**
- The scenario is concrete and specific (five stores, the Al Quoz warehouse, daily sync, the 10% discrepancy, separate terminals, manual balancing).
- It reads as a narrative rather than a formal problem statement. It never states who is affected and what the business impact is (lost sales, overtime cost, customer churn), so the consequences stay vague.
- The figures (10%, 1-2 minutes) are unsourced. If they are assumptions, label them as such.
- The "no loyalty programme" point is a missing feature, not a problem with a stated impact.

**System Capabilities (-0.8)**
- The ten capabilities are clear and map well to the problems. VAT and FTA compliance are a nice touch.
- There is a gap between problem and solution. The problem says managers lack live sales data, but no capability covers sales reporting, dashboards or low-stock alerts. The assignment brief itself names reporting as a core function.
- Benefits mention "reward points" and "warranty lookup", but capabilities only cover applying loyalty discounts and logging serial numbers. Nothing covers enrolling customers or accruing points.
- "Cash, card and credit" is ambiguous (store credit? credit card is already card).
- Manager approval for refunds is mentioned, but no user roles or access control are listed.

**Business Benefits (-0.5)**
- Splitting benefits into tangible and intangible, with metrics, is strong.
- The numbers are unsupported and not traceable. For example, the 35 hours/week saving doesn't reconcile. Saving 40-55 minutes per close, 7 days a week, across 5 branches gives about 23-32 hours if there is one terminal per branch. State the number of terminals and trading days you assumed.
- The ">90% reduction in cash and card tally errors" is an overclaim. Terminal integration removes amount-entry errors on card payments, not cash-handling errors.
- None of the tangible benefits are given a monetary value (AED saved, recovered sales, payback period).
- Training time falling from two weeks to three days is measurable, so it belongs under tangible benefits.
- There is no benefit tied to FTA compliance, such as reduced penalty risk.

## How to reach 9+
1. Add a reporting and dashboard capability with low-stock alerts, and a loyalty enrolment and points capability.
2. Rewrite the problem as a short statement: the problem of X affects Y, the impact is Z.
3. Add a short "assumptions" note covering terminals per branch, trading days and baseline figures, and convert a few benefits into AED.
4. Cite sources, or label estimates clearly, in line with the Harvard referencing requirement.
