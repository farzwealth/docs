## Operational Baseline Assumptions

To ensure complete financial traceability and auditability, all operational calculations and financial figures throughout this System Vision Document are derived from the explicit assumptions defined below.

| Ref # | Parameter / Metric | Baseline Value | Operational Rationale & Source Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | **Retail Store Network** | 5 Physical Stores | Prime Dubai locations: Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, City Centre Deira, Dubai Hills Mall, Dubai Marina Mall. |
| **A2** | **Central Distribution Hub** | 1 Central Facility | Central warehouse located in Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, housing bulk replenishment and logistics. |
| **A3** | **Checkout Terminals** | 2 POS Lanes / Store | Total of **10 active POS checkout terminals** across the five retail locations. |
| **A4** | **Trading Calendar** | 7 Days / Week | Continuous mall retail operations: 365 trading days per calendar year (10:00 AM – 11:00 PM). |
| **A5** | **Overtime Labor Cost** | AED 28.00 / Hour | Blended overtime hourly rate for retail store cashiers and shift supervisors in the UAE retail sector (Bhatia, 2023). |
| **A6** | **Standard Training Cost** | AED 25.00 / Hour | Base hourly onboarding wage for incoming cashier personnel during non-productive training hours. |
| **A7** | **Legacy Closing Time** | 45 Mins / Terminal | Observed historical duration for manual cash drawer counting, card slip tallying, and discrepancy reconciliation. |
| **A8** | **Automated Closing Time** | 10 Mins / Terminal | Target closing duration utilizing integrated payment handshakes and automated drawer float reconciliations. |
| **A9** | **Card Payment Share** | 82% of Total Tenders | In line with UAE market payment trends showing high digital/card adoption over cash transactions (Central Bank of the UAE, 2023). |
| **A10** | **Inventory Inaccuracy** | 10% Discrepancy | Baseline variance between physical store shelf inventory and central inventory ledger caused by batch nightly sync (IHL Group, 2022). |
| **A11** | **Checkout Card Latency** | 1–2 Mins / Customer | Delay attributed to manual cashier entry of total price on standalone EFTPOS machine, key confirmation, and paper cross-checking (NRF, 2023). |
| **A12** | **Annual Retail Turnover** | AED 13,200,000 | Aggregate baseline gross sales across all 5 stores (~AED 220,000 monthly turnover per store). |
| **A13** | **UAE VAT Rate** | 5.0% Standard Rate | Mandated by UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on Value Added Tax (Federal Tax Authority, 2017). |
