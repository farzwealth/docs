---
title: Authorization and future scopes
description: Workspace authorization today and deferred developer-key scope design.
---

The live unified API authorizes a verified Clerk user against the workspace in each private route. Backend membership checks, transaction-local tenant context, scoped foreign keys and forced row-level security protect owned records. Do not infer access from a workspace UUID or from client-side state.

First-party `/api/v1` finance includes both reads and explicit mutations. Its authorization contract is not the earlier site's `accounts:read`/`reports:read` developer-scope matrix.

Granular Paid developer-key read scopes and delegated third-party grants remain deferred. No current documentation promises their issuance, family access,90-day lifetime or revocation behavior. Native private screens and optional provider routes retain their independent gates.

See [authentication](/docs/concepts/authentication) and the [generated route index](/docs/api/endpoints).
