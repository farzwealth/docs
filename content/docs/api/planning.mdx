---
title: Planning, forecast, rent and Zakat
description: Forecasts, cashflow, upcoming obligations, rent, returns, funding comparisons and Zakat estimates.
---

**Status: In testing.** Deployed in the API. Acceptance testing for this area is not complete.

Forecast dates do not prove that a payment was made.

Zakat results are educational estimates. Each result lists its assumptions.
