title: Privacy requests and consents
description: Current unified API contracts, workflow and activation boundaries.
---

Owners can submit access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, consent-withdrawal and human-review requests. Submit the explicit confirmed body with a UUID command key and recent signed authentication. Request history/actions are revision-controlled and append-only; JSON/CSV/HTML downloads are request evidence, not proof that erasure or complete portability has occurred.

Receipt or escalation does not itself erase data, revoke consent, send email or prove legal fulfillment. Country-specific legal deadlines and optional processing approvals require qualified cited review; an internal response target is not a legal deadline. Remaining operator/legal review, retention purge, complete erasure and backup tombstone recovery remain open.

Optional consent grants and withdrawals have their own current-version and authentication rules. A consent checkbox does not configure a provider or supply country approval. See [owned-record exports](/docs/api/owned-records) for the implemented20-group export, which remains narrower than complete portability.
