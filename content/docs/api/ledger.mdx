---
title: Ledger, journal and money records
description: Read the journal, money records, ledger search, pending money and pending transfers.
---

**Status: In testing.** Deployed in the API. Acceptance testing for this area is not complete.

Expected money that has not arrived is kept apart from posted money. Record it as pending, then settle it when the payment is confirmed.

Ledger search returns records that match your filters. Results are paged with a cursor.
