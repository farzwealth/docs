---
title: Accounts and statements
description: Create and read accounts, card statements, statement comparisons and reconciliations.
---

Accounts hold balances in one native currency each. Every amount is a decimal string, and every amount has its currency code next to it. Do not convert amounts to floating-point numbers.

Balance and statement reads take a date. Reconciliation compares the ledger with a balance you enter.
