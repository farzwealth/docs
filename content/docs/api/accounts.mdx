GET accounts returns `AccountResponse[]`, with decimal-string `balance` and separate `currency`; no `items`, `base_balance`, `valuation`, `has_more` or universal Money envelope. It declares no pagination/kind/archive/as-of filters. Account creation returns201 and requires the declared idempotency header. Account status/liquidity mutations require reviewed revisions; do not guess a GET single-account alias.

Private operations require an authorized Clerk user bearer and workspace membership. Optional/sensitive services retain their policy/entitlement/fresh-authentication gates.

| Complete route | Required operation headers (besides authentication) | Declared success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/accounts` | — | 200 application/json AccountResponse[] |
| `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/accounts` | `Idempotency-Key` | 201 application/json AccountResponse |
