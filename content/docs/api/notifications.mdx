The backend supports opt-in preferences, a private inbox, source-backed generation, gated email delivery and signed delivery receipts. In-app and email default off; native push cannot be enabled. The backend is deployed on schema0076, but email bindings are absent and automatic scheduling is not active. Web/native notification controls remain separate work.

### First-party flow

1. GET `/notifications/preferences` under your authorized workspace.
2. PUT the reviewed preferences with `expected_revision` and a UUID `Idempotency-Key`. Choose channels and kinds independently (`due`, `maturity`, `goal`, `reconciliation`). Timezone, local time, quiet hours and advance notice are explicit.
3. POST `/notifications/schedule` with `{"confirmed":true}` and an idempotency key. This explicitly evaluates current sources; it creates no money and reports `external_scheduler_active:false`. Retrying unchanged commands replays the receipt; logical occurrences are deduplicated.
4. GET `/notifications/inbox?limit=25`. Follow its cursor and preserve filters. Pages bind the current snapshot for one hour; changes or expiry require a restart. The default page is25, maximum100.
5. POST `/notifications/actions` with1–100 distinct items, each containing `id`, `expected_revision` and `action` (`read`, `unread`, `dismiss`, `snooze`), plus a UUID command header. Snooze also needs a zoned future `until`, within30days and the item's expiry. The batch is atomic.

Notifications use generic localized text and fixed destinations, not financial amounts or labels. Forecasts are not proof of nonpayment; dismissal changes no source record. Quiet hours and DST affect delivery time, while financial dates retain the workspace calendar. Items leave the visible inbox after30days; this is not physical erasure. Capacity conflicts return409 instead of silently truncating.

### Email boundary

Inspect the safe delivery endpoint for state, attempts and attention. Email requires separately approved operator policy, country/adult scope, reviewed templates, verified contact/domain and private provider credentials. Do not infer delivery from queue admission or a successful schedule receipt. Unknown provider outcomes require reconciliation; they are not automatically resent. Cancellation cannot recall an accepted email. Native push, automatic timer transport, other email families and human linguistic review remain open.

The provider ingress is documented separately under [signed webhooks](/docs/api/webhooks).
