title: Assistant
description: Ask the assistant for help, and confirm, review or undo the actions it prepares.
---

The assistant can ask a clarifying question and prepare an action. Nothing runs until you confirm the action.

After an action runs, you can review it or undo it.

The `reason` field of the availability and response models says why the assistant is not available. `TERMS_UPDATE_REQUIRED` means the stored acceptance of the Terms is older than the current text; accept the current Terms with the [terms-acceptance routes](/docs/api/account-control) and try again.

## Conversations, turns and tool proposals

The chat routes take one turn: `chat` answers in one response and `chat/stream` sends the turn as server-sent events. The conversation routes keep a thread of turns. A queued or running turn can be cancelled, a failed or refused turn can be retried in place, and a turn has its own stream route. The tool routes read data for the assistant (`tools/read`) and prepare changes as proposals. A proposal can be confirmed or undone, an expired proposal can be restaged without a new model call, and a compound call groups several proposals. A change is saved only after you confirm its proposal.

## Hand-off outcomes

When the assistant needs you to act on another screen, such as uploading a statement, confirming records or signing in again, the client opens that screen and then calls `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/assistant/handoff-outcomes` with the `target`, what it believes happened (`claimed`) and up to ten related IDs. The API checks its own state and answers with `verified` and a short `message` in the language you give. Viewing or closing a screen is never a verified result. A claim the state does not back comes back with `verified: false` and the result `not_verified`. The route reads and changes nothing, and a client should send the message as the next turn only when `verified` is `true`.
