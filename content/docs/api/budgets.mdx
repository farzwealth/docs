Budget list/write schemas are endpoint-specific. Posted spending and explicit reversals come from backend finance rules; lending is not budget spending. Do not assume the old generic paginated BudgetItem contract.

Private operations require an authorized Clerk user bearer and workspace membership. Optional/sensitive services retain their policy/entitlement/fresh-authentication gates.

| Complete route | Required operation headers (besides authentication) | Declared success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/budgets` | — | 200 application/json BudgetResponse[] |
| `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/budgets` | `Idempotency-Key` | 200 application/json BudgetResponse |
