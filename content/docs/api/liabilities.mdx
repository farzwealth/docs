Use `/debts`, not the former `/liabilities` alias. Owned liability fractions are independently entered and do not inherit a linked asset share. Actual repayment, dated observations and schedule overrides are distinct commands; an override is not proof of payment or refinancing. Full parameters/revisions and reversal routes are in OpenAPI.

Private operations require an authorized Clerk user bearer and workspace membership. Optional/sensitive services retain their policy/entitlement/fresh-authentication gates.

| Complete route | Required operation headers (besides authentication) | Declared success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts` | — | 200 application/json DebtResponse[] |
| `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts` | `Idempotency-Key` | 200 application/json DebtResponse |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts/{debt_id}/observations` | — | 200 application/json DebtObservationResponse[] |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts/{debt_id}/repayments` | — | 200 application/json DebtRepaymentResponse[] |
| `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts/{debt_id}/repayments` | `Idempotency-Key` | 200 application/json DebtRepaymentResponse |
| `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts/{debt_id}/installment-overrides` | — | 200 application/json DebtInstallmentOverrideResponse[] |
| `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/debts/{debt_id}/installment-overrides` | `Idempotency-Key` | 200 application/json DebtInstallmentOverrideResponse |
