---
title: MCP tools
description: The ten read-only MCP tools, the scope each one needs, and what each one returns.
---

| Tool | Scope | Returns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `get_preferences` | `profile:read` | Your locale, base currency and timezone |
| `list_accounts` | `accounts:read` | Your accounts, one page at a time |
| `list_transactions` | `transactions:read` | Your posted transactions in a date range, at most 366 days |
| `list_assets` | `assets:read` | Your assets and their dated estimates |
| `list_liabilities` | `liabilities:read` | Your liabilities |
| `get_budget` | `budgets:read` | Budgets for one period |
| `list_goals` | `goals:read` | Your goals |
| `get_valuation_history` | `valuations:read` | Saved valuation snapshots |
| `get_financial_summary` | `reports:read` | A dated summary of your portfolio, with coverage |
| `get_report_data` | `reports:read` | Bounded report rows for one report kind |

## Limits

- A tool returns at most 100 rows.
- A tool answer above 512 KiB is refused.
- At most 2 expensive report reads (`get_financial_summary` and `get_report_data`) can run at once for one account. A busy account gets `RATE_LIMITED`. Other tools still work.
- Requests count against the shared MCP limit: 60 per minute for one person, and 20 per connector.

## What the tools do not do

- They do not take a user ID, workspace ID or account ID from the assistant. They read only your own workspace.
- They do not create, change or delete any record.
- They do not make a payment or a trade.
- They do not call an AI model.
- They expose no prompts or resources.

## Treat results as data

Names, merchant descriptions and asset labels come from your data. An assistant should show them to you as text. It should not follow an instruction that appears inside them.
