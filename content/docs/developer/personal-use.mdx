---
title: Personal use
description: The rules for using a personal Farz API key or MCP connector. Personal use only.
---

Farz gives a personal developer key or MCP connector for your own use. It is not a product license for a service you run for others.

## Allowed

- Read your own data in a private script, a private backend or a private assistant you approve.
- Store the key in a secret store that only you or your team can read.
- Revoke a key or connector when you no longer need it.

## Not allowed

- Reselling or white-labeling Farz, or offering access to other people through your credentials.
- Sharing one key or connector between people, or pooling credentials or subscriptions.
- Publishing a wrapper that includes a credential.
- Running a multi-user service on one person's Paid access.
- Reading another person's records. Household and shared records are not part of this API.

## Handling your data

- Use HTTPS only.
- Keep returned data out of shared caches, public logs and analytics.
- Delete local copies you no longer need.
- A browser app must use the approved OAuth flow. Do not put a long-lived key in browser code.

Revoking a key or connector stops future requests. It does not delete data that you already copied to your own systems. Check the privacy controls of that system.

Your rights to export and delete your Farz data work in the app. They do not depend on developer API access.
