---
title: Features not offered
description: Features that are not part of the Farz plan. Do not expect them.
---

These features are not offered, and they are not on the roadmap. The API does not include them, and the apps do not have them.

- **Household, family or shared workspaces.** Each workspace has one owner.
- **Members, invitations, roles and accountant access.** There is no way to invite someone into your workspace.
- **Guardian features.** Farz does not offer a guardian role or a guardian-managed account.
- **Phone numbers as a contact method.** Farz does not use a phone number to contact you.
- **Direct bank connections.** Farz does not connect to your bank account.
- **OCR.** Farz does not run optical character recognition on your documents.

If a route in the API reference mentions a household, member or guardian concept, that clause does not apply to your account.
