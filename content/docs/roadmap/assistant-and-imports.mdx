## Assistant chat

**Status: In testing.** The assistant is in alpha for one named tester. Usage has a monthly cap. The assistant can prepare an action, and nothing runs until you confirm it.

Planned: a wider release after the beta. No date is set.

## Statement import for CSV and XLSX

**Status: In testing.** The import pipeline was deployed on 28 October 2026. It has not yet been proven with a real file end to end in production. The steps are upload, scan, parse, review and commit. Nothing is written to your ledger until you commit.

## Statement reading for PDF and photos

**Status: Planned.** Not deployed. Until it is built, a PDF or photo import answers that you should enter the rows manually.

Reading a statement from a photo or a PDF needs a page-preparation step and a worker. Neither is built yet.

Optical character recognition is not part of the plan.
