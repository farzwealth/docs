## Plans

Farz has three plan states: Free, Trial and Paid. A Trial lasts 30 days and can be started once per verified email.

## How payments are planned

- **Web:** Stripe, once the business licence is in place.
- **Ziina:** planned as a payment method.
- **Android:** Google Play and RevenueCat, for in-app purchases.

Prices are not set in these docs. Do not rely on a price shown in any route or screen until Farz announces it.

## What follows payments

Tax, VAT and invoices are planned to be reviewed before the number of users nears the level where tax applies. That review is part of the payments work.

## Until payments are live

- Paid features stay off. The developer API and MCP need a Paid plan or a staff-granted beta access grant.
- Staff can grant beta access for up to 90 days. A beta grant is not a payment and creates no billing record.
