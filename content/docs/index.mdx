## Executive Overview

Welcome to the **System Vision Document (SVD)** and operational specification repository for the **Multi-Store Retail & Central Warehouse Management Platform**. 

This system unifies five retail store locations in Dubai with a central distribution hub at **Al Quoz Industrial Area**, eliminating inventory data lag, modernizing point-of-sale checkout through integrated payment terminals, and enforcing strict regulatory compliance under UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) mandates.

<Callout type="info" title="Academic & Industry Alignment">
  This documentation addresses the requirements for **Deliverable 1: System Vision Document**, incorporating rigorous problem formulation, an exhaustive capabilities matrix, traceable baseline assumptions, a mathematical financial return model in AED, and Harvard-formatted references.
</Callout>
